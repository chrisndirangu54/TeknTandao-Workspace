Hybrid network topology

A MikroTik subscriber edge connects the upstream provider to a GPON fiber access network and two point-to-point wireless-fed buildings. Central management and analytics are logically separated from customer traffic.

Illustrative topology. Exact splitter loss, RF link budget, VLAN interoperability and hardware management interfaces require lab testing and site surveys.

Smarter service operations

TeknTandao's integration roadmap prioritizes tangible reliability gains rather than speculative autonomous control.

📡Proactive monitoring

Collect approved device telemetry and end-to-end probes to correlate faults, detect unusual packet loss and highlight affected sites.

🔐Managed customer access

Subscriber authentication through PPPoE and RADIUS, optical provisioning through compatible OLT/ONT systems and TR-069 via GenieACS.

💳M-Pesa billing design

Plan verified Daraja settlement, payment reconciliation, subscription renewals and audit trails before enabling production transactions.

🧠AI-supported diagnosis

Link equipment alarms, incidents and site topology to generate evidence-based recommendations for human operators.

🛠️Technician workflow

Register faults, acknowledge incidents, create work orders and track completion, with accountable operator actions.

📊Customer experience analytics

Future dashboards can connect support outcomes, measured availability and renewal patterns to service improvements.
